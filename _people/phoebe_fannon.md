It ended up being a pretty similar setup to the 2020-2023 system - an Axia Livewire core with Zetta/GSelector playout/scheduling supported by a questionable collection of Windows workstations and Linux virtual machines. Albeit in far nicer, soundproof digs than before! This is the system that remains in operation today.

I have many fond memories of URN from my 3 years at Nottingham, and I shall attempt to come up with some specific examples in due course.
