Analog Archives deep-dives genres, musical movements and artists every week to bring 'hidden' information to our listeners. We've done everything and anything from Riot Grrrl to Transphobia in Rock to The Women Behind Bob Dylan. We have made a zine of our first season (year) on-air, this can be found on our Instagram @analog.archives.urn or in person (copies to be released soon). 

(mostly) Jenni designed our weekly posters that were posted on socials, and we both curated the playlists on Spotify. 

Inês brought back the Live Lounge sessions, bringing in student artists to perform on-air.
Jenni and Inês did a urn1350 Instagram take-over for the Uon v. NTU Battle of the Bands, Live and Loud (2026) hosted at the Level, organised by NTU SEMP and UoN BandSoc.
We did a number of collaboration episodes with other URN members from all areas of the Station - News, Daytime, Sports, After Dark.
